# Phishing and Impersonation

Do not share passwords, one-time verification codes, recovery codes, or full payment details with anyone claiming to be support. Support will not ask you to disclose a one-time verification code in a chat or email.

If you received a suspicious message, avoid its links and attachments. Preserve the sender address and message headers when possible, then report the message to support. If you already shared credentials or a code, change the account password immediately and follow the account-compromise steps.
