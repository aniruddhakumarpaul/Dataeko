# Duplicate Charges

If two charges have the same amount and relate to the same subscription period, first check whether one is a temporary authorization and the other is the completed charge. Temporary authorizations may disappear automatically according to the card issuer's timing.

If both charges have fully posted, send support the invoice number, charge dates, and last four digits of the payment method. Do not send the full card number or CVV. Support can investigate a duplicate-charge refund after confirming the billing records.
