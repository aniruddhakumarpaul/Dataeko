# Suspicious or Unrecognized Login

If you see a login, device, or MFA prompt you do not recognize, treat the account as potentially compromised. Change the password from a trusted device, sign out other active sessions if that option is available, and review security settings for unfamiliar changes.

Do not approve unexpected MFA prompts or share verification codes. Report the approximate time, device/location shown, and any other unusual account activity to support without sending secrets or recovery codes.
