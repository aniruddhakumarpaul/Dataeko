# Suspected Account Compromise

If you believe someone else accessed the account or changed credentials without permission, change the password from a trusted device immediately and sign out other active sessions where available. Review the account email, security settings, connected devices, and recent activity for changes you did not make.

Do not share passwords, recovery codes, or one-time verification codes with support. Report the approximate time of the suspicious activity and any unauthorized changes so the incident can be escalated for human review.
