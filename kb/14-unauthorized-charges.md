# Unauthorized or Fraudulent Charges

If a charge is not yours or you believe payment details were used without permission, treat it as a security and fraud issue rather than a normal billing dispute. Contact your payment provider promptly using the number or channel on the official statement or card.

Also report the charge to support with the date, amount, invoice or transaction reference if available, and only the last four digits of the payment method. Never send a full card number, CVV, password, or one-time code.
